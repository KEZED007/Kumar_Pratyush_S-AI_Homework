Question Title: Deployment Project Permissions
Question Link:
https://my.newtonschool.co/playground/code/3wz3wv7c89fo?lecture_hash=vifkrugjcwth

Solution:
chmod 700 deployment/app/*.py
chmod 770 deployment/datasets/*
chmod 770 deployment/datasets
chmod 644 deployment/docs/OVERVIEW.md
chmod 755 deployment/bin/*.sh

Appraoch:
1. First, I used the chmod 700 command to give the owner full permissions for all Python files.
2. Then, I used chmod 770 to give the owner and group full permissions to the datasets directory.
3. I used chmod 770 again to give the owner and group full permissions to all files inside the datasets directory.
4. Next, I used chmod 644 to give the owner read and write permissions and the group and others read-only access to OVERVIEW.md.
5. Finally, I used chmod 755 to give the owner full permissions and the group and others read and execute permissions for all shell scripts.
